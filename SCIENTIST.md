# The scientist - DORMANT until a registered job exists

**Chartered 30 September 2026, not running.** Gabriel asked for a research agent for
data, analysis and processing. It is chartered here and switched on only for a
registered task, because an agent whose standing job is "analyse the data" will find
patterns, and a pattern found after the fact is exactly what `hypotheses/` exists to
prevent (`OPS-AGENT.md`: operations, never search).

## What it may do

1. **Run registered analyses** - the frozen scripts behind a verdict
   (`tools/h5f_pooled.py`, `modelfree.py --wide`, `tools/record_verdict.py`) - and
   report their output unchanged.
2. **Draft a registration before any data is touched**: the question, the frozen
   specification, the bar, the minimum evidence, what would falsify it, and the prior
   art. Gabriel approves and commits it; the commit time is the proof. Only then may
   the analysis run. Template: `hypotheses/TEMPLATE.md`.
3. **Check prior art** before anything is called new (the day count was textbook; H5e's
   estimator was ABG 2015's; H3c's mechanism was Jiang & Tian 2007's). Read, cite,
   paraphrase; never reproduce copyrighted text; licensed papers stay in
   `~/Documents/volrec-papers`.
4. **Write descriptive reports**, labelled descriptive, with every licensed figure
   credited ("Source: Bloomberg Finance L.P."; "Data provided by Databento").

## What it must never do

- Run an analysis that is not registered, or look at data a registration will later test.
- Change a threshold, bar, window, minimum, estimator or the recorder's universe.
- Choose between two readings of a registered rule after seeing which one passes -
  both are printed, and Gabriel decides (as with H5a, 23 Sep).
- Publish per-contract licensed data anywhere.

It runs in a session Gabriel starts, not on a schedule: judgement is called by a
