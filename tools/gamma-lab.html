Distribution of net profit and loss across 300 simulated paths. One path is luck. The centre of this distribution is the edge.

Reading it. Set realized above implied and the average lands positive — you bought movement cheaply and got paid for it. Set realized below implied and it goes negative: you paid for movement that never showed up, and time decay eats you. Set them equal and the average sits near zero, which is the whole point. The option's price already contains a forecast; you only profit when reality beats the forecast.

What's simplified. Interest rates are zero, no transaction costs, no bid-ask spread, no dividends, and volatility is constant rather than moving around. Real desks fight all four, and costs are precisely why you can't re-hedge infinitely often even though the maths says tighter hedging tracks better. This is a learning instrument, not a trading system.
