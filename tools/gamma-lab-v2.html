Sweep — how often should you re-hedge?

In v1 the answer was always "as often as possible." With costs, it isn't. This runs the whole simulation at every rebalance interval from daily to every 20 days and finds where net profit peaks.

Average net profit and loss by rebalance interval, 150 paths each.

Where real data plugs in. Everything above is generated by makePath() in the script. Replace that one function with a real price series and a real implied-vol series and the rest of the engine works unchanged — the P&L accounting, greeks, and cost model don't care where the numbers came from. That's the seam.

Still simplified. Zero interest rates, one strike, no skew (real implied vol differs by strike, and that shape matters), no early assignment, no borrow cost on the short shares — and borrow is a real expense that can swamp thin edges in hard-to-borrow names.
